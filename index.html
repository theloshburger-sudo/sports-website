Rules Q&A

New here? Open any question for a plain-language answer.

?What are BragBoard coins?

Coins are earned game rewards, not money. You start with zero. They cannot be purchased, transferred, cashed out, or exchanged for anything of value.

?How do I earn coins?

When coins are enabled, each weekly winner earns 10 coins. Each monthly winner earns another 20. Tied winners each receive the full reward. Rewards are applied once when the demo results are finalized—not when you submit picks.

?How do power-ups work?

If your league enables them, spend 10 earned coins to boost one game before locking your weekly card. A correct boosted pick earns 2 points instead of 1; an incorrect pick earns 0. You can activate only one boost per week. It is committed immediately and does not let you change a locked pick.

?Are pretend-pot chips the same as coins?

No. Play chips are free, display-only counters showing a pretend group pot. They do not affect scores, buy power-ups, or pay out to winners. BragBoard coins are earned separately.

?How do weekly picks and the monthly winner work?

Choose exactly one team for every displayed game, then lock your card. A correct pick earns 1 point, or 2 for a boosted game. Points add up across four demo weeks. The highest monthly total wins. Buying a boost spends coins, not points.

?Can I change my picks after locking?

No. Locked picks stay visible but cannot be edited. The next demo week gives you a new card. This demo uses manual locking and simulated results; it is not connected to real kickoff deadlines or live scores.

?What happens if players tie?

Tied weekly and monthly winners each get the full coin reward. For punishments, everyone tied at the losing cutoff is included. For example, bottom three can include a fourth person tied with third-lowest place.

?What does Approve all punishments do?

It marks every currently submitted proposal as approved by you. It does not approve for other members, submit your own proposal, approve league settings, or cast a selection vote. Review the proposals first. You can withdraw an individual approval, and edits to a proposal clear its earlier approvals.

?Why are approval and voting separate?

Approval decides which punishments everyone considers acceptable. Only unanimously approved options enter selection. A vote, wheel spin, or snake draft then chooses from that pool. A selection vote does not make an unapproved punishment eligible.

?What is shared versus personal punishment?

Shared means the same approved punishment applies to anyone in the chosen losing positions. Personal means each member gets a specific assignment before the month begins. Personal mode needs at least one distinct unanimously approved proposal per member.

?Who has to do the punishment?

Your settings choose last place or bottom three in the monthly standings. Punishments remain voluntary: anyone may opt out without losing points or coins. Dangerous, degrading, sexual, illegal, or financially coercive proposals are not allowed. The group reviews safety; the app does not certify it.

?What can the host decide?

The host prepares the league settings. Everyone Votes requires every member to approve the settings and final assignments. Host Decides lets the host confirm settings and start the month. Neither mode bypasses unanimous approval of eligible punishments.

?How do selection ties and snake drafts work?

Selection-vote ties use the displayed proposal order. Personal voting assigns choices in roster order without repeats. Snake drafting uses forward then reverse roster order for two rounds. Shared drafts use the most selections; personal drafts assign the highest available preference in roster order.

?How do I demo a group without switching 30 times?

Click Join as the last player to load 30 simulated members and an empty proposal field for you. After submitting, click Simulate the other 30 responding for their approvals and votes. Your approvals and ballot stay manual. During play, Fill & lock other sample cards handles the other players.

?Is this a real online league?

Not yet. Members, approvals, invites, fixtures, and results are simulated on one device. No messages or payments are sent. Refreshing or resetting clears your progress. The simulator is for trying the game flow, not proof of real member consent.
