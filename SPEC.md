# TARGET: today's build

Choose the idea, person, interaction, and visual direction. The agent can help phrase and save your decisions after you approve them. The provided scope and review safeguards stay in place.

- **Thing:** BragBoard — a one-page real-team sample pick’em with private-group punishment setup, weekly picks, monthly standings, and optional non-cash coins.
- **Audience:** Groups of friends who watch pro sports and want a simple, free season-long bragging-rights competition without fantasy-roster complexity or real-money stakes.
- **Requirements:** Every sample member proposes a harmless voluntary punishment. Only unanimously approved proposals enter selection. Choose shared or personal assignments, voting, wheel, or snake draft, and last place or bottom three before play. Lock exactly one winner per displayed game. Award 10 coins to weekly winners and 20 to monthly winners in explicitly simulated results; optionally spend 10 coins on one double-point pick per week before locking. Coins cannot be purchased, transferred, redeemed, or exchanged for value. Preserve sport-specific animations.
- **Guardrails:** Static browser code. No required external service, keys, accounts, runtime AI, or private data. Label fictional or sample content. Preserve the example and publishing setup. Work on a branch and wait for human review before shipping.
- **Experience:** Accurately numbered ten-question rounded onboarding (name, decision authority, one league preference at a time, custom punishment proposal), with high-contrast selectable cards and Back/Continue navigation. Every setup question and option has a keyboard- and touch-operable explanation, alongside a first-time how-to-play introduction. Custom proposals use a blank textarea with no templates, suggestions, autocomplete, or automatic safety verdict. Everyone Votes requires unanimous settings and final-assignment approval; Host Decides reserves settings edits to the sample host without overriding unanimous punishment consent.  A website-first, sporty dark-mode interface inspired by Sleeper’s bold clarity: condensed display type, black/charcoal panels, gold accents, subtle tiger-stripe texture, large game cards, and a prominent private-league leaderboard.
- **Test:** Exercise proposal and unanimous-approval gates, shared and personal selection, vote/wheel/snake order, visible assignments and voluntary opt-out, weekly pick immutability, one-time simulated rewards, and pre-lock power-up limits. Clearly label all sample members and results; verify the registered Pages URL only after approval and merge.
